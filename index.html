Gmail Local Agent

A personal, single-user tool that runs on the developer's own computer and reads the developer's own Gmail mailbox through the Gmail API, using the read-only scope (gmail.readonly), to search messages and download attachments.

  • Used only by the developer, with their own Google account.
  • Runs locally. There is no server, website, or hosted service.
  • Lists, reads, and searches email and saves attachments to local disk.
